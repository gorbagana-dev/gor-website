BRAND & PRESS

Welcome to the Gorbagana Brand & Press page. When communicating Gorbagana in marketing—including advertisements, articles, websites, and print—please follow these guidelines.

Decentralization Without Permission. Gorbagana is a satirical Solana fork born from the "unbranded chain" debate. Our brand reflects technical precision with an irreverent trash aesthetic, celebrating our community-first, anti-establishment philosophy.

Clearspace

A certain amount of space is needed around the logo to prevent it from becoming cluttered by surrounding artwork, images, or the edge of a page. The minimum clearspace equals the cap height of the "G" in GORBAGANA.

Typography

Primary Typeface

Pusia Bold is the primary typeface for Gorbagana branding. It's a modular, geometric typeface with sharp, uniform letterforms inspired by 1980s tech aesthetics.

GORBAGANA

Font-family: Pusia, Inter, -apple-system
Weight: Bold (700), ExtraBold (800)
Styling: ALL CAPS, tracking -0.02em

Secondary Typeface

Logo Don'ts

Here are some things you should never do with the Gorbagana logomark to maintain brand integrity.

✕

Don't add shadows

Keep logo marks clean without shadows or gradients

✕

Don't add outlines

Maintain the original logo design

✕

Don't stretch the logo

Always maintain proper aspect ratio

✕

Don't apply in low resolution

Use high-quality versions only

✕

Don't use as a frame

Don't place imagery inside the logo

✕

Don't use low contrast

Must pass WCAG AA contrast standards
